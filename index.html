02 · Revenue protection & risk

Dispute & Chargeback Management

Established and led dispute resolution operations across PayPal, Shopify and Adyen. Improved the PayPal dispute win rate from 35.3% to 63.6% following a process change, monitored over 60 days.

PayPalChargebacksProcess improvement
Explore case study

Challenge: Payment disputes create financial exposure and require consistent coordination between customer service and dispute resolution teams.

My role: Established and managed the dispute resolution team, implemented process improvements, and monitored dispute performance. Developed customer service escalation guidance to support case handling and communication.

Result: The PayPal win rate rose from 35.3% to 63.6% during the 60-day monitoring period following the process change — an improvement of 28.3 percentage points.

Business value: Stronger dispute handling, clearer case ownership, and improved revenue protection. The result is a PayPal dispute win-rate measure, not the overall chargeback incidence rate or a dollar recovery claim.
